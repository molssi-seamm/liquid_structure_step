liquid_structure_step.analysis module#

The structural analysis of the Liquid Structure step, independent of SEAMM.

  • Species and sites: each molecule is named by its formula, and a site is an element of a species (“O(H2O)”), an element in every species (“O”), or an MDAnalysis selection (“select: name O and resname 1”).

  • Radial distribution functions accumulated frame by frame, with errors from blocks of frames – the independent runs, or blocks of a single run; the first peak and minimum; coordination numbers.

  • The classification of cation-anion pairs as free, solvent-separated (SSIP), contact (CIP) or aggregated (AGG), from distances only, so it also works without a periodic cell.

The pairs of points of a and b within cutoff, with their distances, under the minimum image convention in a periodic box.

As MDAnalysis’ capped_distance, but for a rectangular box (or none) it uses scipy’s periodic k-d tree, about ten times faster for the dense liquids here: 4.4 million pairs within 12 Å for 9,436 atoms took 5 s a frame (SEAMM job 5276, 2026-10-20). liquid_structure_step.analysis.peak_and_minimum(r, g, width=0.2, sustain=0.2)[source]#

The first peak and first minimum of a g(r).

The g(r) is smoothed over width Å, and a crossing of 1 counts only when it lasts sustain Å, so that the noise of a sparse pair (a few ions) is not taken for structure. The first peak is the highest point (of the unsmoothed g(r)) of the first stretch above 1; the first minimum the lowest point of the smoothed g(r) between it and the next stretch above 1 – unless that is far_beyond() the peak, when it is the first clear dip after the peak if there is one (see _first_dip()).

Returns:

peak and peak_height, minimum and minimum_height; None where they cannot be found.

Return type:

dict

liquid_structure_step.analysis.residence_time(members, dt, max_bytes=67108864)[source]#

The residence time of solvent molecules in shells, ps: the time at which the normalized autocorrelation of the occupancy, <h(0) h(t)> / <h>, first falls below 1/e (interpolated), or None if it does not within half the run.

The autocorrelation is found with FFTs, a batch of pairs at a time so that the memory is bounded by about max_bytes. Its sums are of products of zeros and ones, so they are rounded to the integers they are.

Parameters:

  • members ([set of (solute, solvent molecule)] or [numpy.ndarray of int]) – The pairs in contact in each frame, as pairs or integer codes.

  • dt (float) – The interval between frames, ps.

  • max_bytes (int) – The memory for a batch of pairs.

liquid_structure_step.liquid_structure module#

Non-graphical part of the Liquid Structure step in a SEAMM flowchart

The structure of liquids and solutions from the positions sampled in MD: partial radial distribution functions and coordination numbers, the classification of ion pairs (free, solvent-separated, contact, aggregated), and the solvation shells of solutes (coordination numbers per solvent, the shells’ composition, pairing and residence times). The analysis itself is in liquid_structure_step.analysis.

The MD is run before this step – in a Loop for independent runs – or read from files; the step analyzes it. The errors are from the independent runs, or from blocks of a single run.
